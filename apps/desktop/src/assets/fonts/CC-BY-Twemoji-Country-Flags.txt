twemoji-country-flags.woff2

"Twemoji Country Flags" font, from the country-flag-emoji-polyfill project
(https://github.com/talkjs/country-flag-emoji-polyfill, MIT, Copyright (c) 2022 TalkJS).

The flag artwork inside the font comes from Twemoji
(https://github.com/twitter/twemoji), Copyright 2020 Twitter, Inc and other contributors,
used and redistributed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license
(CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode

The font is bundled unmodified. It is used only for flag characters (regional indicator
symbols), so flags look the same on every operating system.
