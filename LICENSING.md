## The Spiral app

Spiral's code is public and free to use, modify and learn from. It's licensed under the Functional Source License (FSL), so you can't use it to build a competing product. Each version becomes Apache-2.0 open source two years after it is released.

This is "source-available" (also called "Fair Source"), not open source, until a version's two years are up.

## The registry and `.spk` format

`apps/registry` and `libs/spk-format` are Apache-2.0 open source, so other tools can adopt the item format and run their own registry freely.
